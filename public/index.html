About This Directory

This tool helps you find which floor and aisle a subject area is shelved on at John K. King. Type a subject — like Military History, Poetry, or Architecture — and press Search or hit Enter. You can also narrow results by floor using the dropdown next to the search bar.

We do not catalog individual books — this is a Subject Directory to help you find where a subject is shelved. Having trouble finding your topic? Ask a staff member — we may still have it even if it's not listed here.

Tip: search broad subjects, not specific topics. Instead of "World War II submarines," try "Military History."

Directory Key

📄 P.B.
Paperback
📕 H.C.
Hardcover
🔚 End Cap
A single shelf mounted on the end panel of a bookcase, facing the aisle perpendicularly — like a cap on the end of a row
📚 Case
One bookcase unit — a single column of shelves within a larger row. An aisle may have many cases side by side
↔️ Center
The main aisle running the full length of the floor, dividing it into East and West halves

🧭 Finding Your Way

⬆️ North
The front of the building — the stairwell wall and the Lafayette Blvd. entrance
⬇️ South
The back of the building — the rear wall where the elevator is
⬅️ West
The 5th Street side of the building
➡️ East
The parking lot side of the building

The words Front, Rear, East, and West may appear in the Room/Description column of the directory to help pinpoint where on a floor a section is located.

What This Does Not Do

This directory does not search for specific books, titles, or authors. It only shows where subject categories are located in the store. To find a specific book, ask a staff member in person.
